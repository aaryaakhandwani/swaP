Our Story

Built by pet lovers, for pet lovers

swaP was born from a simple frustration — why is there no permanent, verified digital identity for our pets? We decided to build it.

Vision

A world where every pet has a permanent digital identity

We envision an India where every dog, cat, and pet — regardless of breed or owner — is registered, verified, and connected to a living digital record from the day they arrive in a family.

Mission

Connect the entire pet ecosystem on one trusted platform

Our mission is to bridge pet parents, veterinarians, breeders, and housing societies through verified data — making pet healthcare and management effortless and trustworthy across India.

What swaP Does

The digital backbone for India's ₹20,000 crore pet industry

swaP creates permanent digital vaults for pets — storing medical records, vaccination history, identity certificates, and QR tags. Accessible by anyone, anywhere, with a single scan.
