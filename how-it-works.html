How swaP works

From registration to a fully managed pet profile — it only takes three simple steps.

Get Started Free
01
Step One

Register your pet

Create your swaP account, choose your user type — Pet Parent, Breeder, Vet Clinic, or RWA Society — and complete your pet's profile. Add name, breed, age, and upload a photo.

Select user role Choose pet type (Dog / Cat / Others) Upload health documents Set vaccination schedule
02
Step Two

Receive your QR tag

We generate a unique, tamper-proof QR tag linked to your pet's digital vault. It arrives within 3–5 business days. Attach it to your pet's collar — it's their passport to all services.

Personalised QR code Shipped to your door Waterproof & durable Instant scan access
03
Step Three

Access all services

Use your dashboard to book vet appointments, share records with clinics, view vaccination reminders, and manage your pet's entire health journey from one place — forever.

Book vet appointments Share records securely Vaccination reminders Emergency QR access
